M MERCY GX

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · effective 9 November 2026 · applies to Mercy GX for Android, the desktop build and the gamehub web app
Short version

Mercy GX does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising SDK. Everything the app remembers — history, bookmarks, game scores, settings — stays in your own device's storage.

1. Who we are

Mercy GX ("the app") is a web browser built by Sk Inventor (individual developer, India). Contact: skiventoroffical@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

DataWhereWhy
Browsing historyDevice storage onlySo you can see and revisit pages
BookmarksDevice storage onlySaved pages you chose
Game high scoresDevice storage onlyGamehub best-score display
SettingsDevice storage onlyYour preferences persist between launches
Cookies / site dataDevice storage onlyStandard browser behaviour for sites you visit

None of the above is uploaded anywhere by the app. It is deleted when you use "Clear browsing data" or uninstall the app.

3. What we do not do

  • No account, login or email collection.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reports or usage statistics.
  • No advertising SDK and no ad tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No background uploads of your browsing activity.

4. Permissions the app asks for

PermissionPurpose
InternetRequired to load web pages — that is what a browser does
Network stateTo show the online/offline indicator
Storage (Android 9 and older only)To save files you download into your Downloads folder

5. Websites you visit

When you browse, the sites you open receive standard technical information (IP address, browser user agent, cookies) exactly as in any browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control and are not responsible for. Mercy GX does not add tracking of its own on top.

6. Search

The address bar sends your query to whichever search engine you use — by default Google. If you add an optional third-party search API key (for example Serper) in the web app, the query goes to that provider under their terms; the key is stored only on your device.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Your control

  • Clear everything: menu → Clear browsing data.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the app — all local data goes with it.
  • Questions or deletion requests: email the address above.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that sends data anywhere (for example sync or crash reporting), this page will be updated first and the version number above will change. Material changes will be noted in the release notes.
